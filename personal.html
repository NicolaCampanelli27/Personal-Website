Training

I fell for CrossFit first, then powerlifting and circuit training, and later triathlon and climbing. Fitness became a big part of my identity and I have no intention to quit despite everything.

The Mountains

Hiking has always been there. From our family holidays in the Alps, through 8 years of being a Scout (once a Scout, always a Scout), to discovering climbing. Can't wait to be back.

Introspection

I have always talked a lot with myself. That is not necessarily an advantage, the other way round if something. But I truly believe a soliloquy could be life-changing. Reading good books is usually a great tool: most of the answers we look for in life have been already addressed by someone else.

Right now: a knee injury has kept me away from sport for about two and a half years. I'm still working (and hoping) on getting back, slowly and with much more patience than I used to have.
