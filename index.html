Teaching a hybrid car to save fuel

I trained a reinforcement learning agent, by its typical environment exploration, when a plug-in hybrid should use its engine or its battery (what is called an Energy Management System).

Fuel use delta above the mathematical best (defined with Dynamic Programming) on the standard WLTC test cycle when deployed Real-Time!

Publications

Two papers from my time as a researcher.

2026

Optimal control and techno-economic analysis of long-duration battery energy storage system applications

Nicola Campanelli and Matilde D'Arpino. Journal of Energy Storage, vol. 164, 2026. The Ohio State University.

Combines optimal control with a cost analysis to judge whether long-duration batteries pay off for a commercial building and a wind turbine. Bills drop by up to 20.4% with a battery alone and 32.4% with wind, but current storage costs are still too high to be profitable.

2024

Development of a Soft-Actor Critic Reinforcement Learning Algorithm for the Energy Management of a Hybrid Electric Vehicle

Luciano Rolando, Nicola Campanelli, Luigi Tresca, Luca Pulvirenti and Federico Millo. SAE Technical Paper 2024-37-0011. Politecnico di Torino.

A reinforcement learning controller for a diesel plug-in hybrid. It came within 8.6% of the theoretical optimum on the WLTC, and on two cycles it had never seen, the gap ranged from 1.9% lower to 17.5% higher fuel use.
